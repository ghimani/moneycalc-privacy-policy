Privacy Policy for MoneyCalc

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyCalc ("the app") handles information when you use the app. MoneyCalc provides calculation tools. The app does not offer user registration or login.

1. Information stored by MoneyCalc

MoneyCalc may store calculator history and related calculation information locally on your device so that you can view it within the app. This locally stored history is not uploaded by MoneyCalc to our own server or to a cloud account.

You can remove locally stored information by using any clear-history option provided in the app, clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored history.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

MoneyCalc displays advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), a service provided by Google. The SDK may automatically collect and share certain information to deliver, measure and improve advertising, help prevent fraud, and maintain the advertising service. Depending on the SDK version and configuration, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers, where applicable;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, stability and energy-use information.

This information may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their own policies and settings. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Learn more:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Android
  • How Google uses information from apps or sites that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Information we do not request through MoneyCalc

MoneyCalc does not provide account creation or login. The app does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics, based on its current configuration. MoneyCalc does not send locally stored calculator history to a MoneyCalc-operated server.

4. Data sharing

MoneyCalc does not sell your locally stored calculator history. Information handled by the AdMob SDK may be transmitted to Google and its service providers or advertising partners as needed to provide advertising and related services. Please review Google's policies linked above for details about Google's handling of information.

5. Data security and retention

Calculator history is retained locally on your device until you clear it, clear the app's storage/data, or uninstall the app. The app does not maintain a separate cloud copy of this history. Data handled by AdMob is subject to Google's security practices; Google indicates that Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

6. Children's privacy

MoneyCalc is a general-purpose calculator app and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. If you believe that information has been provided to the app inappropriately, please contact the developer using the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, SDKs, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage, together with its effective date.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy-related requests about MoneyCalc, please contact the developer using the developer contact email shown on the MoneyCalc Google Play Store listing.
